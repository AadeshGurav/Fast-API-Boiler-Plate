# Code of Conduct

This project adopts the [Contributor Covenant, version 2.1](https://www.contributor-covenant.org/version/2/1/code_of_conduct/).
The short version:

## Our pledge

We want taking part in this project to be a harassment-free experience for everyone,
regardless of age, body size, visible or invisible disability, ethnicity, sex characteristics,
gender identity and expression, level of experience, education, socio-economic status,
nationality, personal appearance, race, caste, colour, religion, or sexual identity and
orientation.

## Expected behaviour

- Be kind and assume good intent; most mistakes are accidental.
- Give and accept constructive feedback about code, not about people.
- Help newcomers: answer the question they asked, point to the docs, make the next step clear.
- Respect people's time and differing viewpoints; disagree in the open, decide on the evidence.

## Unacceptable behaviour

- Harassment, insults, or derogatory comments, in public or in private.
- Sexualised language or imagery, and unwelcome attention of any kind.
- Publishing others' private information without their explicit permission.
- Trolling, deliberate disruption, or any conduct that would be inappropriate in a professional setting.

## Scope and enforcement

This applies in all project spaces (issues, pull requests, discussions, reviews) and when
representing the project elsewhere. Report problems to the maintainer,
[@AadeshGurav](https://github.com/AadeshGurav), or through GitHub's
[content reporting](https://docs.github.com/en/communities/maintaining-your-safety-on-github/reporting-abuse-or-spam).
Reports are handled promptly and confidentially. Maintainers may edit or remove contributions
and temporarily or permanently ban participants who break this code, following the
Contributor Covenant's enforcement guidelines (correction, warning, temporary ban, permanent ban).
